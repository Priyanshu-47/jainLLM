Subject: Help requested — identifying authors/publishers/rights contacts for three historical Sthānakavāsī publications (1941–1960)

Respected Sir/Sadhvi ji / Committee members,

Namaskar. I am a volunteer with a small non-commercial open-source project building a study and research assistant for the **Śvetāmbara Sthānakavāsī Jain tradition** — a tool that helps students find and study Jain teachings with full source citation and provenance. It preserves the distinction between canonical texts, commentaries, expositions and modern teachings, and is not intended to replace religious authorities.

Public digital libraries hold copies of three historical works from the Conference's milieu. Their library records do not name an author, speaker or publisher, which prevents both scholarly citation and any lawful use. We are seeking help **identifying the works and their current rights holders or the appropriate rights contacts** — we are not asking anyone to confirm ownership of them.

1. ***Sthanakvasi Jainonum Dharamkartavya*** (1960, Gujarati) — a lay-conduct manual. *Questions: who was the author? who published it? who would hold or manage its rights today, or who should we approach?*

2. ***A.B. Shree Swetambar Sthanakvasi Jain Conference no Chadati Padatino Itihas*** (1941, Gujarati) — an institutional history of the Conference. *Questions: who compiled/authored it? was it published by the Conference itself? does the Conference hold, or know who holds, rights in its historical publications?*

3. ***Adhyatmik Pravachno Part-1*** (1960, Gujarati) — a discourse (pravachan) collection; the speaker is unnamed in the record. *Questions: who was the speaker? who published the collection? who should we approach about its rights?*

Separately, and only as a question: **does the Conference claim any rights or custodial authority over its own historical publications** (for example the 1941 institutional history), or is there a person or office that manages such matters?

If you are not the right contact for these questions, we would be equally grateful for a pointer to whoever is.

Any answer will be recorded in our public project catalogue with its date and source. If, later on, a rights holder is identified and a permission conversation becomes possible, we would ask separate, clearly-scoped questions (research use, retrieval with attribution, model training, redistribution, derived works) — each one optional for the grantor.
