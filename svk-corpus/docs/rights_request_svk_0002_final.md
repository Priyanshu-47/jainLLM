Subject: Permission request and rights-holder identification — *Jain Dharma* (1958) by Muni Sushil Kumar, A.B.S. Sthanakvasi Jain Conference Bhavan, New Delhi

Respected Trustees / Managing Committee, Siddhachalam — International Mahavira Jain Mission,

Namaskar. I am a volunteer with a small non-commercial open-source research project building a **study and research assistant for the Śvetāmbara Sthānakavāsī Jain tradition**. The system helps students and researchers locate and study Jain teachings, and always cites the work, edition and page a passage comes from. It keeps canonical texts, commentaries, expositions and modern teachings in separate, labelled layers, preserves full provenance for every passage, and is not intended to replace religious authorities or the living tradition.

I am writing about one work connected with your founder:

**Muni (later Ācārya) Sushil Kumar, *Jain Dharma*** — published by the A.B.S. Sthanakvasi Jain Conference Bhavan, New Delhi, in **1958**.

We would like to make this work accessible to students through our project, but we will not proceed without permission from whoever controls its rights. Indian copyright terms mean the work is protected today, and the fact that a library copy is publicly viewable is not, in our understanding, permission.

**First, two questions about authority — before any permission is discussed:**

1. Are you the appropriate person or institution to identify the **current rights holder** of this 1958 work (for example the author's estate, heirs, lineage, or the original publisher)? If not, could you point us to the right contact?
2. Does the International Mahavira Jain Mission or Siddhachalam **claim any rights or custodial authority** over this work or over Ācārya Sushil Kumar's literary works generally? We ask without assuming any such claim.

**If a permission conversation becomes possible**, each of the following is a separate question, and a "yes" to one is not a request for the others. We are glad to accept a narrower permission — item (A) alone would already be valuable:

- **A. Reading/research use** — that we may transcribe and process the text for study and research;
- **B. Retrieval use (RAG)** — that the text may be used in a reference system that quotes short passages **with full attribution**;
- **C. Model-training use** — that the text may be included in training data for the research assistant (we will not assume this right);
- **D. Redistribution** — that the transcribed text may be published openly with your attribution;
- **E. Commercial use** — we do not seek it; we record a position only if you wish to state one;
- **F. Derived works** — that translations or annotations may be produced as research derivatives.

Any permission would be recorded exactly as granted — scope, conditions, attribution requirements, restrictions, the name and authority of the grantor, and the date — and our systems honour those conditions automatically. If attribution wording or placement matters to you, please tell us and we will follow it.

If it is easier to respond by indicating the correct rights contact and nothing further, that alone would be a great help.
