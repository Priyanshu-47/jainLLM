Subject: Permission request — non-commercial academic project on Śvetāmbara Sthānakavāsī Jain literature (works: *Jain Dharma*, 1958, and related Conference publications)

Respected Sir/Sadhvi ji / Committee members,

Namaskar. I write on behalf of a small open-source research project building a **study and research assistant for the Śvetāmbara Sthānakavāsī Jain tradition**. The project is volunteer-run and non-commercial. Its purpose is narrow and respectful of the tradition:

- to help students and researchers find and study Jain texts through a question-answering system that **always cites the source** a passage comes from;
- to **preserve provenance** — for every passage, the work, edition, page and publisher are recorded;
- to **distinguish layers of knowledge** — canonical sūtras, commentaries, expositions such as your publications, and modern teachings are kept separate and never blended;
- **not** to replace religious authorities, ācāryas, or the living tradition. The system only points students back to the sources.

We are writing about four works connected to the A.B.S. Sthanakvasi Jain Conference, copies of which are held in public digital libraries:

1. **Muni Sushil Kumar, *Jain Dharma*** (A.B.S. Sthanakvasi Jain Conference Bhavan, New Delhi, 1958);
2. ***Sthanakvasi Jainonum Dharamkartavya*** (1960, author and publisher not identified in the library record);
3. ***A.B. Shree Swetambar Sthanakvasi Jain Conference no Chadati Padatino Itihas*** (1941, institutional history);
4. ***Adhyatmik Pravachno Part-1*** (1960, speaker and publisher not identified).

We do not know whether the Conference owns the rights to these works, represents their rights-holders, or whether the rights-holders are others (for example, the families or lineages of the authors). We would be grateful if you could tell us:

- whether the Conference **owns or controls** rights in any of these works;
- whether it **represents** a rights-holder who could grant permission;
- whether it **can grant permission** itself; or
- **who currently controls the rights**, if not the Conference.

**What we are asking permission for.** Each item below is separate, and a "yes" to one is not a request to agree to the others. We happily accept a narrower permission — even permission for item (A) alone would be valuable to us:

- **A. Reading/research use** — that we may transcribe/process the text of the work(s) for study and research;
- **B. Retrieval use (RAG)** — that the transcribed text may be used in a search/reference system that quotes short passages **with full attribution**;
- **C. Model-training use** — that the text may be included in training data for the research assistant (this is a separate right and we will not assume it);
- **D. Redistribution** — that the transcribed text dataset may be published openly (e.g., on Hugging Face/GitHub) with your attribution;
- **E. Commercial use** — we do not seek this; the project is non-commercial, but if you wish to state a position we will record it;
- **F. Derived works** — that translations or annotations of the text may be produced as research derivatives.

Any permission would be recorded with its exact scope, conditions, the name and authority of the person granting it, and the date — and the project's systems honour such conditions automatically. If you prefer, we can restrict redistribution to attribution-only, keep texts out of public downloads entirely, or limit use to (A) and (B).

We would also welcome correction: if anything in our understanding of these works' publication history is wrong, we will record the correction in our public catalogue.

With respect and thanks,
