## 2. Why Task 26 Search Strategy Was Too Narrow

Task 26's approach had two problems:
1. **Keyword limitation**: Searching only for "Sthānakavāsī" misses sources that are Sthanakvasi-relevant but don't contain that literal word
2. **Tradition collapse**: Treating all results as either "Sthanakvasi" or "not Sthanakvasi" ignores the rich hierarchy: Jain → Svetambara → Sthanakvasi → teacher → practice
